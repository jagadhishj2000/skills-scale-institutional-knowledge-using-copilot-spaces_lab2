# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured, iterative project management approach focused on customer value, clear ownership, and data-informed decisions. Our process is built on principles of psychological safety, transparency, and continuous improvement.

## Key Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named Project Manager and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning

## Project Lifecycle

OctoAcme projects follow a five-phase lifecycle:

### 1. Initiation

Validate business need and align stakeholders.

- **Guide**: [Project Initiation Guide](octoacme-project-initiation.md)
- Define problem statement, goals, and success metrics
- Identify stakeholders and resources
- Make go/no-go decision

### 2. Planning

Turn approved initiatives into actionable plans.

- **Guide**: [Project Planning](octoacme-project-planning.md)
- Break work into shippable increments
- Define acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Identify dependencies and risks

### 3. Execution & Tracking

Manage day-to-day delivery and track progress.

- **Guide**: [Execution & Tracking](octoacme-execution-and-tracking.md)
- Daily standups and weekly syncs
- Quality and testing standards
- Risk escalation and blocker management

### 4. Release & Deployment

Standardize release processes and reduce risk.

- **Guide**: [Release & Deployment Guide](octoacme-release-and-deployment.md)
- Pre-release requirements and deployment checklist
- Rollback and incident playbook
- Release notes and communication

### 5. Close & Retrospective

Capture learnings and drive continuous improvement.

- **Guide**: [Retrospective & Continuous Improvement](octoacme-retrospective-and-continuous-improvement.md)
- Run structured retrospectives
- Convert learnings into action items
- Measure improvement impact

## Core Roles

For detailed role definitions and responsibilities, see [OctoAcme Personas](octoacme-roles-and-personas.md):

- **Project Manager**: Coordinates delivery, schedules, risks, and communications
- **Product Manager**: Defines outcomes, prioritizes backlog, measures success
- **Developers**: Implement features and collaborate on design
- **QA/Testing**: Validates quality and acceptance criteria
